Install with **Not a Twig 0.2.2** (both are needed for the sync; each still works alone).

**One meal schedule across both apps.** With Not a Twig installed, its meal slots are the schedule: every timed slot (Lunch 1:00, Pre-workout 4:30, Supper 7:30) has a routine meal due at that time here, keeping that meal's own lead-in and how long until it gets angry. Your 7:00 "Pre-workout meal" now sits at 4:30 with the plan's meal time moved with it. A new slot in Twig shows up here as a meal; a meal here with no Twig slot is paused (listed on the Routine page), not deleted.

Change a meal's time in either app and the other follows: edits here are written to Twig straight away; edits in Twig arrive when it tells Not a Fraud, and the day's plan moves its meal blocks. Deleting a linked meal here removes that slot in Twig. Uninstall Twig and every paused meal comes back on its old time.
