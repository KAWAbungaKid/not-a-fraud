Install over 0.2 like before. Your data carries over.

**Planning**
- **7 days ahead.** The Plan tab has Today plus the next 6 days. Later days are drafts that firm up each morning; Google Calendar shows them too, marked "(draft)".
- **Kinds of work** (task editor → Kind):
  - *Steps + waits*: laundry, research runs. Only your attended minutes are booked; the waits run in the background and the next step is placed when they end ("On time" = exactly then). Check a step off, or the whole thing.
  - *Practice*: skills or catch-up study. A weekly target, sprinkled into gaps, one session a day, up to the longest-session length.
  - *Errand*, *Waiting on* (never planned until you switch it back), *Low-brain* (preferred when tired or in short gaps and late evening).
- **Importance 1–5** per area (Map → area) and per task, plus **Bump** (+1 for a week). Research starts at 5, School at 4.
- **Catch up** practice for each class, 3 h a week each. Change or delete them like any task.
- **Tap any block on the Plan tab**: Start, Done (or "this step is done"), Later today, Not today (warns if it's due today), Pick a day, Something came up, Bump. Classes and weekly blocks: **Skip and make it up later**, or just skip.
- **How's today?** (Now tab, replaces "Rough day?"): Push · Recharge then push (20 / 45 / 90 min) · Reset · Write it off, with a suggestion based on your sleep, skipped blocks and the time of day.

**Calendar**
- Each calendar is **Events**, **Deadlines** or **Ignore** (More → Google Calendar). Canvas-looking calendars default to Deadlines: every assignment becomes a task due then, updates when Canvas changes, and disappears when it's removed.
- Exams in your calendar get study tasks automatically. Delete one and it stays deleted.

**Blocking**
- **Calls are never blocked** (phone, Snapchat, FaceTime-style, WhatsApp).
- **Tap a Snapchat notification** → Snapchat opens free for 5 minutes. **Typing keeps it open.** When it ends: "Still texting: 3 more minutes".
- **Quick Snap tile**: add it to your quick settings; 5 free minutes to film and send, 6 a day.
- **Credits now wait 60 s** before opening (kills the scroll urge). **Emergency is instant**, no phrase, 3 a day.
- Every part of this is adjustable per app: More → Blocking → Snapchat rules / Rules for every other blocked app.
- Inbox removed.

**Routine and sleep**
- Night sleep and naps are separate (Now: "6.2h +20m").
- Wake-up fills in from your watch, or from your first unlock after 4am when you didn't wear it, and shows on time or late.
- Bedtime nudges play a short sound on the alarm channel, so they get through Do Not Disturb and Bedtime mode (More → Blocking to turn off).
- Workouts: climbing, sports, hikes, swims count toward 6 once you've done 3 standard ones that week.
- Old web-app tasks wait in a **review** card on the Now tab: Keep / Done / Delete.

**After installing:** Android may switch the guard off because its settings changed (it now also watches for the keyboard during reply windows). If so, More → Setup row 2. Then add the Quick Snap tile.
