- **Scan a label:** in a new food, tap "Scan label" (camera) or "From photo". The phone reads the Nutrition Facts panel on-device (nothing is uploaded) and fills in serving size, calories, protein / carbs / fat and the rest of the label. Check every number, add the name, save.
- **Log in any unit:** servings, count units (egg, slice, scoop, tortilla…), g / oz / lb / kg, and ml / tsp / tbsp / fl oz / cup / pint / l. Switching units keeps the same amount of food. The app remembers the unit you used last for each food.
- Serving sizes like "2/3 cup (55 g)" fill grams and volume automatically; foods can have their own count units ("1 breast = 2 servings"). Existing foods were upgraded.
- Entries, meals and recipes show what you typed ("8 oz", "3 eggs").
