Install over 0.2.2 (data stays). Health Connect will ask once more, for **sleep**.

- **Sleep:** Twig reads last night's sleep from Health Connect itself. A short night (under 6.5 h) adds a bad-day suggestion, Insights compares 7 h+ nights with shorter ones for clarity and energy, and the weekly review shows your average.
- **Plan tab (meal planner):** plans the rest of today from what you actually eat: your saved meals, recipes and foods you've logged twice, at your usual amounts. It aims at today's calories with protein covered. Shuffle for another plan, "Cheaper" to lean on price, and "Log this plan" to put it in the diary. **Grocery list** for the next 3, 5 or 7 days.
- **Price per serving** (optional) on any food, for the planner and the grocery total.
- **Scan barcode:** in a new food, photograph the barcode. The phone reads it, then one lookup on Open Food Facts fills in the name, brand and label. Check it against the package before saving. Not every product is there; scan the label for those.
- **Weekly review (Monday):** last week in one card on Progress and a Monday notification. It covers complete days, weigh-ins, intake vs target, protein, the trend, your measured maintenance, sleep, energy and clarity, plus one suggested change.
- **Experiments (Progress):** pick a question with two conditions (chocolate milk 15 min before vs a meal 3 h before the gym; 16:8 vs 12:12). Days alternate A/B; Today shows the day's condition with "Did it / Didn't" and an optional "workout felt" rating. Results compare energy, clarity and workouts per condition, and say how sure they are.
