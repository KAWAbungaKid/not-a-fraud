Install over 0.3.1 like before. If something's badly broken, reinstall 0.3.1 from the releases page (your data stays).

**New "You" tab** (replaces Routine in the bottom bar): Routine | Stats | Skills.
- **Stats**: Body, Mind, Work, Hygiene, Discipline, Social, Spirit, 1–10 over the last 7 days, with the week-before trend. Tap one to see what it's made of. A stat under 4 makes its areas one step more important in the plan (a task's own importance still wins; switch in More → Planning). "Pushed back most" lists what you defer, just to notice.
- **Weekly check-in** (Stats tab, or a card on Now on weekends): four 1–5 taps for people, faith, calm and happiness. Social and Spirit come from this.
- **Skill trees**, Skyrim-style: Athletics (Backflip → Flair), Climbing, Music (Piano), Magic (Card tricks) to start. Tap a node: level, hours, progress to the next level (1 / 5 / 15 / 40 / 100 h). "Plan practice" gives it weekly time in the planner; "Start now" times it; you can add minutes done away from the phone. A child skill opens once its parent hits level 1. Add your own trees and skills; mark a tree as a sport and 20+ minutes counts as the day's workout.

**Planning**
- **Auto re-plan**: when a planned block passes and you didn't work it, the rest of the day re-plans. Anything that no longer fits shows on Now under "Moved off today".
- **"Won't fit this week"**: work due within 7 days that the week doesn't have room for.
- Practice blocks size themselves to how long you actually last (after 3 sessions). The task editor shows it, and how often a task was pushed back.
- Recharge ideas are editable (More → Planning).

**Running low**
- Two or more signs (short nights, up late, skipped blocks, phone time way up, missed meals, no workout in 4 days) → one notification a day and "How's today?" opens with the signs listed.

**Blocking**
- **Calls**: 0.3 trusted Android's "in communication" audio mode, which some apps leave on after a call or use for voice notes. That switched blocking off (likely your Snapchat misses). Now a VoIP call only counts while the microphone is in use.
- While an app is open for free (reply window, Quick Snap, still texting) a notification says so with a countdown.
- Message reply window is 10 minutes after a new message (was 15).
- **More → "Why wasn't I blocked?"**: the last 200 blocking decisions with reasons. If Snapchat gets through, look there.

**Look**: accent color picker (More → Look).
