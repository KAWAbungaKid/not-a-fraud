Install over 0.9.1 (data stays). New page: **More → Brick ladder**. Start by picking your **Level 3 apps** there.

**The brick ladder.** The phone gets less usable the further the day drifts, and comes back down when you do things, not by waiting. Points come from today only (every morning starts at 0): slipped planned blocks, overdue routine items, time in blocked apps, a must-do workout not started. 25+ minutes of work takes points off; any running session puts it back to Normal.
- **Level 1, Friction:** credits wait twice as long; "what are you doing?" asks twice as often.
- **Level 2, Dull:** credits wait 3×, grayscale (needs the computer step), unlocking lands on Now first.
- **Level 3, Brick:** only your Level 3 apps open, credits freeze.
- **Bedtime** (10:00 pm–5:30 am, adjustable) is Level 3 by itself, plus bedtime extras like your alarm clock.
- Always open: calls, texts, Settings, your three apps, focus apps, Snap reply windows, emergencies.
- A notification says why it climbed and the way back down. Protected time (date night, Sunday off) never goes past Friction.
- **Home screen widget** (long-press home → Widgets → Not a Fraud) and an optional **lock screen wallpaper** with the level, what to do now and today's 3.
- Level 3 starts only once you've saved your Level 3 list. Suggestions come from what's usually needed and what you use; blocked apps are marked.

**Confirm before changing.** Every setting now asks "Change X from A to B?" first. Anything that loosens still waits for the morning, and now asks you **why**; in the morning your reason is shown next to "still want this?". Deleting or switching off a routine item waits the same way.

**Medium settings lock.** The guard now covers the phone's Settings pages that would switch Not a Fraud off: Uninstall / Force stop, its accessibility switch, notification and usage access, battery limits. Opening one steps back and offers a 10-minute window the next morning. Turning things ON is never stopped; the rest of Settings works as usual. Safe Mode remains the emergency exit.

**Computer step (optional), explained on the Brick ladder page:** one command lets the app switch grayscale and turn its own guard back on if it's ever switched off. Grayscale it turned on is the only grayscale it turns off, so your morning routine is left alone.

**Skip several.** Tap a class or block in the Plan → "Skip several times or until a date…", with or without make-up tasks (trading through ME 3411, for example).
