- **Planned unlock.** Brick ladder page (and "Planned unlock…" on the block screen): switch things off from now, or from a later time, until a date and time to the minute ("until Wed Dec 3, 5:15 PM"). Pick what goes off each time: the brick ladder and bedtime (any app opens, new ones too), your blocked apps and sites, the unlock check-in, routine and plan nags.
- **Not for weak moments:** you type a long phrase word for word (pasting is off) and say why; the reason stays on screen the whole time. At most 31 days at once. Ending early or cancelling is instant; extending means setting up a new one (phrase again). The phrase can be changed in Setup, but the change waits until the next morning.
- Active and upcoming unlocks show on Now with "End it now".
