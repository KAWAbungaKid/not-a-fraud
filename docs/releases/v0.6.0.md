Install over 0.5.0 like before (data stays). Rollback: reinstall 0.5.0 from the releases page.

**To-do list** (More → To-do; put it in the bottom bar under Look & tabs)
- Four levels: **ASAP** (front of the plan), **on a day**, **within dates** (next 3 days, this week, 2 weeks, this month, or pick), **try eventually** (kept on the list, never planned until you move it up).
- Tap an item to change its level, length, or open full details.

**Homework calendars**
- New calendar type **Homework** (More → Calendar & health). Canvas calendars switch to it automatically; existing Canvas tasks moved over.
- Every assignment starts at 2 h. On the Plan, each day lists what's **due** that day: tap one and say how long it'll really take. You can also tap a planned block → "How long will this take?". The sync keeps your number.
- Big assignments are split: at most 2 h a day on one assignment (more only if the date needs it).
- **Working ahead:** each assignment aims to be done 2 days before it's due. Now shows a Homework card: when the next unfinished one is due, how much is left before the "ahead" line, and the plan works that in first. Once you're ahead, it keeps planning 2 days out so you stay there.
- All adjustable under More → Planning: default length, days ahead, most per day, how early it may start.

**Settings lock** (on by default; More → Blocking & credits)
- Anything that loosens blocking waits until **7:00 the next morning**, then asks "Still want this?" (card on Now + a notification). Examples: turning blocking or nudges off, unblocking an app or site, longer windows, more emergencies, a new focus app, a new emergency phrase, turning the lock itself off.
- Tightening applies right away. Waiting changes are listed there, and you can cancel them any time.

**"What are you doing?" (YouTube, on by default)**
- Opening YouTube asks what you're opening it for. Type it and it opens for 5 minutes, then asks again: "Still on YouTube. What are you doing now?" Today's answers stay on that screen, so "scrolling" sits right under "how to fix bike chain".
- Up to 60 min a day this way, then it's credits or emergency. Turn it on for any app and change the 5 / 60 in that app's rules.

**Emergencies**: when the instant ones run out, you get more (5 by default), but each one means typing your phrase ("I am choosing this on purpose") out in full. Adjustable per app.

**Blocked apps**: only installed apps are listed (TikTok etc. are gone). New **Suggested to block**: apps averaging 20+ min a day this week that aren't blocked, with Block / Not this.
