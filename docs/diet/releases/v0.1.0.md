First build of **Not a Twig**, the diet app (installs next to Not a Fraud and Not a Troglodyte).

- Food diary by meal (Lunch / Pre-workout / Supper / Snacks — rename them in Settings), recent foods, saved meals and recipes, "Same as yesterday".
- Starter list of staples (chicken, rice, eggs, oats, milk…), approximate; copy real labels in as you go.
- Targets for a recomp at 6'0" / 200 lb / ~13.5 %: ~3,144 kcal on training days, 190 P / 70 F, carbs the rest; "Where these numbers come from" shows the math.
- Daily weight → trend line; after ~2 weeks of weigh-ins + complete days it measures YOUR maintenance and takes over from the formula.
- Intermittent fasting window (12–8 by default), time since food, food outside the window recorded.
- 2-tap check-in (brain fog, energy) + basic suggestions on a bad day; Progress compares fasting / protein / water days against how you felt once there's enough data.
- Nags for weigh-in, meals, water (with a "+16 oz" button on the notification), check-in.
- Reads Samsung Health active calories (Settings → Connect) and Not a Troglodyte's workouts.
