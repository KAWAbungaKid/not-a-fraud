**Feedback mode.** Turn it on in More → Modules & feedback. A small round button floats on every page (drag it out of the way). Tap it: it takes a screenshot of what you are looking at, notes the page, and asks what you would change (type it, or use the keyboard mic). Notes stay on the phone. When you are done, More → Modules & feedback → **Share feedback** makes one zip (all notes + screenshots) to attach in chat with Claude, or save to Drive. **Clear** deletes them after.
