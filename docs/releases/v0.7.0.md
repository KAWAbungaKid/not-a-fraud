Install over 0.6.0 like before (data stays). Rollback: reinstall 0.6.0 from the releases page.

**Homework fix**
- 0.6 skipped calendar entries that were marked "free", or from a calendar hidden in Google Calendar. Canvas entries often are, so nothing reached the plan. Homework, Deadlines and Tests calendars now read every entry; Events calendars still skip free / hidden ones.
- All-day assignments were dated a day early. Fixed.
- More → Calendar & health now shows, for each calendar, how many entries it found in the next 60 days and how many homework tasks it made, and warns if a calendar isn't syncing to the phone.

**One priority per task**
- Must / High / Normal / Low (or "Area" = the area's level). "Pin as hot" and "Bump for a week" are gone: pinned tasks moved up one level, an active bump too.
- Pick 3 is above all of it for that day. Firm deadlines still climb on their own as they close in.
- Set it in the task editor, from a block on the Plan, or for a whole area on the Map (hold the area).

**Tasks page**
- Tabs: **Map · Projects · To-do** (the old "List" is now Projects; To-do moved here from More).
- Map: **tap** opens a goal / area / project; **hold** to edit, rename, link or archive; every opened node has a **+** to add the next thing (goal → area → project → task). Tap a task to edit it.
- Projects: **+ Task** and **+ Project** at the top.
- "Capture an idea" is gone. Your saved ideas are now "Try eventually" to-dos.

**Workout app**
- Now shows this week's workouts against your minimum (3) and the full plan (6), today's suggested workout and a **Start workout** button.
- The plan labels today's workout block with the suggested day ("Workout: Push") and drops the block on days you've already trained.
- Your workout routine item ticks itself when the workout app says today's is done.
- **When today has to be a workout** (every day left is needed for the minimum): from noon, a nudge every hour (louder after 6 pm), and **credits can't open blocked apps until the workout is started**. Emergencies, reply windows and "what are you doing?" still work. Short mode counts.
- If the week's minimum can't be made any more: one honest note, then quiet until next week.
- Settings: More → Planning → Workout app (the credit pause is under the settings lock).
