- **Unlock check-in.** While something's overdue, unlocking shows one screen with quick answers: late routine items ("Did it", "Did it earlier", "Open it" for your Bible app), missed plan blocks ("I worked on…", "Something else", "Nothing, honestly"), Not a Twig's weigh-in (type it right there) and meals ("the usual", or a few words plus when you ate), and the "you said this mattered" item. **Later** hides it 20 minutes; 3 Laters in a row add a ladder point until you answer one. Never during a call; Later is always there. Switch: Brick ladder page.
- **Plan shows planned vs actual.** Past blocks keep their colour: green done, blue "did something else instead", amber partly, red missed, grey explained. Tap a past block to say what you did. Top line: planned so far, on plan, worked.
- **Day score on Now** (work vs plan 50 %, routine 30 %, blocked apps 20 %) and **good days in a row** (70+).
- **"You said this mattered."** A high-priority / hot / exam task due within 4 days with under 30 min in 3 days shows in red on Now and in the check-in: "3h on trading today · Study for Thursday's test: 0 so far, ~3h left."
- **End-of-day summary** in the evening (and yesterday's next morning until you tap Got it).
- **I worked on… / I went over.** Log time at a past time (off the phone, forgot Start, trading ran long); the rest of the day re-plans and what doesn't fit moves to the coming days. Hot work that no longer fits while date night or Sunday off is still ahead asks before taking that time.
- **"Lab is done."** During a class / lab / trading block: end it early; the freed time gets planned and Now suggests what to fill it with.
- **Clearer Now buttons.** Task block: "Start working" / "Finished it". Class or lab: "<class> is done" / "Work on <task>". Routine line: "Routine overdue: … ✓ Did it". (Before, "Start" next to a lab started the top task, which was confusing.)
- **Lock screen** (Brick ladder → Lock screen wallpaper): also shows what's due or late to log, the next thing, and today's missed blocks.
