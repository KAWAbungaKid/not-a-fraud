Install over 0.1 (same as before: download the APK, install). Your tasks and credits carry over.

**New**
- **Map** (Tasks tab → Map): you in the middle, your 6 goals, the areas that feed each goal, projects, and tasks (tap a project to open its tasks). Tap anything to rename, link, add under it, archive. Dashed lines = an area feeds more than one goal. List view is still there (Tasks → List), now with search.
- **Routine tab**: wake, fluids, cardio, shower, vitamins + creatine, three meals, workout (6 a week, climbing counts), pick tomorrow's 3, teeth, bed. Each item gets angrier the longer it's put off — one nudge when late, then every 30 min when it's really late. Teeth also fires when you plug in or turn the screen off for the night. Tap any item to change its times. Wake-up and workouts fill in from Samsung Health when they can.
- **Google Calendar**: everything in your Google calendars is a fixed event the plan works around. The app writes your plan into a "Not a Fraud" calendar so the whole day shows in Google Calendar. One-time setup in More → Google Calendar.
- **Website blocking**: instagram.com, tiktok.com, x.com, reddit.com, youtube.com, facebook.com are blocked in Chrome, Samsung Internet and other browsers, same credits and unlocks as apps. Edit the list in More → Blocking.
- **Pick 3**: from 5pm the Now tab asks for tomorrow's three. They're planned first and starred.
- **Trading** is a hard block every weekday at 8:30. On Tue/Thu the app adds a "Make up ME 3411 class" task.
- **Weekly blocks** can be edited (times, days, firmness) in More → Weekly blocks. Habit blocks can give way when a high-priority deadline is under a day away; the plan says when that happens.
- **Exams in your calendar** without a study task get a one-tap "Add study task".
- Brighter secondary text.
