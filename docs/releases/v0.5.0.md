Install over 0.4.0 like before (data stays). Rollback: reinstall 0.4.0 from the releases page.

**Pages instead of long scrolling**
- More is now a menu. Every settings group has its own page: Setup checks, Blocking & credits, Calendar & health, Weekly blocks, Planning, Projects, Look & tabs, Data & about.
- Routine, Stats, Skills and Classes are their own pages (the "You" tab is gone). Pick which 4 go in the bottom bar under More → Look & tabs; the rest are one tap away in More. Back goes back.
- Choose which cards show on Now (Look & tabs). Phone screen time moved to Stats.

**Classes & semesters**
- New Classes page: semester name, first/last day, days off (single dates or "date to date"). Every class follows those dates.
- Add/edit a class: meeting times (add a second time for a lab), and weekly study time the planner schedules for you.
- **End semester**: removes class times and archives the classes you check. Their tasks and logged time stay, but nothing in an archived project gets planned. Restore under More → Projects.

**Skill trees look like Skyrim**: each tree is a constellation in a night sky; swipe between them. Stars get bigger and brighter with level, lines light up as paths open, the tree grows upward. Tap a star for its details.

**Samsung Health sleep**
- 0.4 only read anything if sleep, exercise *and* steps were all allowed; now each is read on its own. The last 3 days are filled in, not just today, and the app asks for Health Connect's background permission so the hourly sync can read too.
- More → Calendar & health shows exactly what Health Connect has: what's allowed, and every sleep record of the last 2 days with the app that wrote it. If it says "No sleep records", Samsung Health isn't writing to Health Connect: Samsung Health → ⋮ → Settings → Health Connect → allow Sleep.

**Less hard-coded, less duplicated**
- Routine items: "Checks itself when…" (wake up / a workout is logged / you pick tomorrow's 3) on any item, instead of three built-in items. Yes, picking your 3 already ticked "Pick tomorrow's 3"; now any item can be the one.
- Areas (Tasks → Map → tap an area): set its importance 1–5 and which stat it counts toward.
- Priority (high/normal/low) overlapped importance. It's gone from the task editor: importance 1–5 is how much a task matters, and tasks with a due date have a "Firm deadline" switch. Old low/high settings were folded into importance.
- Blocked-app rules are listed per blocked app, not just Snapchat.
- Removed the old .ics and web-app import buttons (Google Calendar replaced them).
- Weekly check-in is off (tabled); Stats shows only what the phone measures on its own. Switch it back on under Planning.
