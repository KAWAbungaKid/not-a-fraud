Install over 0.10.0 (data stays).

**One practice type.** Every recurring practice is now a practice task: a weekly target, a longest session, and (new) an optional **fixed weekly time**. Your Sunday CSWE practice and scout manual blocks were converted, same times, so the plan looks the same; their time now counts toward the task. Set one up in Tasks (kind "practice"), or from a skill's "Plan practice".

**Workouts.**
- On a tight week (one spare day left) the workout block stays in the plan even on a Recharge / Reset day, like a must-do day.
- A week whose minimum is already missed costs 2 points of Discipline.
- The Body stat counts Not a Troglodyte's own record of finished workouts.
- **Climbing tomorrow:** Not a Fraud tells Not a Troglodyte when tomorrow has a sport (a planned sport-skill block, or a calendar entry like "climbing"). The Troglodyte side arrives in its own release.

**Blocking.**
- YouTube (and Instagram, Reddit…) in a browser now uses that app's rules, so "what are you doing?" asks in Chrome too.
- **What it was for:** Blocking & credits lists every answer from the last 7 days per app, with how many were "just scrolling". On Sunday evenings a note reminds you to look.

**Stats.** Discipline now also counts time in blocked apps (compared with last week) and how far ahead your homework is.

**Look → Text contrast:** Normal, Brighter or Brightest for secondary text.

**Skills: draw your own constellation.** Edit a tree → "Draw your own constellation…", then tap dots on the grid to join them. Skills become stars along your lines. Every level you earn scatters more stars along it, so the shape fills in as you go.
