Install over 0.11.0 (data stays). Nothing is set up for you; add it to the routine items you want.

**Routine items can open an app when you unlock.** Edit a routine item → **Open an app when I unlock** → pick the app (your Bible app, say). Then choose:
- **Open it once it's** due, late, or angry.
- **Only when the brick ladder is at least** any level / Friction / Dull / Brick. Gentle at first, pushier as the day slips.
- **Open it again after** 5 / 15 / 30 / 60 min, if you back out of it.
- **Tick it after this long in the app** (5–30 min), or leave it ticked by hand.

While the item is due and not done, unlocking the phone takes you straight into that app instead of the home screen (never during a call, never after quiet time). The app always opens, even at Level 3 or bedtime. Time spent in it counts toward the item. The routine row says "opens … on unlock" so you can see which items do it.
